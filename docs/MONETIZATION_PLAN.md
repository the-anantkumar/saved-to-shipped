# Deferred paid work

Paid subscriptions are outside the free-beta and open-source release scope. The implementation and its original plan are preserved on `feat/firebase-backend` at [`da7652b`](https://github.com/the-anantkumar/saved-to-shipped/tree/da7652bc0919b4d7cc1a04a2c627cb01bc6b926e).

The active [free beta](FREE_BETA.md) has no checkout, paid tier, monthly organization points, card requirement or trial expiry. It retains technical storage/work limits and uses each member's own optional provider accounts. Merchant setup and pricing do not block this release.

Historical product/payment evidence records the earlier branch, not the free-beta artifact. Revisit paid work as a separate reviewed feature after the beta's UX and usefulness are accepted.
